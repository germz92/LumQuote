Payments

How clients can pay invoices online

Credit Card Surcharge

The fee is added as its own line item in checkout ("Card processing fee") so clients see exactly what they're paying. It is never applied to bank (ACH) payments — card networks require that, and it encourages clients to pick the cheaper option.
3% roughly covers Stripe's card fee (2.9% + 30¢). Some states restrict surcharging above ~3% — check your local rules.

Bank Transfers (ACH)

Clients connect their bank account through Stripe at checkout. ACH costs far less than cards (0.8%, capped at $5), but funds take a few business days to clear — invoices are marked paid automatically once the transfer settles (requires the Stripe webhook to include the checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded event).
